## Salesforce Functional Capability Matrix

### 1. First Understand What “Functional Capability” Means

Very important term.

Let us break it slowly.

---

#### Functional

Means:

A specific business function or department.

Example:

- sales
- marketing
- support
- finance

---

#### Capability

Means:

What a system can do.

Example:

- send emails
- manage leads
- automate support
- generate reports

---

#### Matrix

Means:

Organized structure showing multiple capabilities together.

---

### 2. So What Is Salesforce Functional Capability Matrix?

It means:

The different business functions Salesforce can handle using its products and platform.

In simple language:

Salesforce helps different departments inside a company perform their work efficiently.

---

### 3. Why Businesses Need This

A modern company has many departments.

Example:

| Department | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Marketing | Attract customers |
| Sales | Sell products |
| Service | Support customers |
| IT | Build systems |
| Management | Analyze reports |

Problem:

Before integrated platforms:

Every department used separate disconnected software.

Result:

- duplicate data
- poor communication
- slow operations
- inconsistent customer experience

Salesforce solves this by connecting everything together.

---

### 4. Big Picture Understanding

Salesforce tries to create:

One connected business platform.

Where:

- marketing
- sales
- support
- analytics
- AI
- integrations

all work together.

---

### 5. Main Functional Areas In Salesforce

Salesforce capabilities mainly include:

- Marketing
- Sales & E-commerce
- Services
- Communities
- IoT & AI
- Platform
- Integration
- Support

We will understand each one slowly using real world examples.
