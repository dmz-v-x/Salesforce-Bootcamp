## What is CRM?

### 1. Full Form of CRM

CRM stands for:

Customer Relationship Management

It is both:

- A business strategy
- A software/system

used by companies to manage their relationships with customers.

---

### 2. Simple Real-World Understanding of CRM

Imagine you own a shop.

You have customers who:

- buy products
- ask questions
- complain about issues
- request support
- come back again later

Now imagine you store everything about customers in notebooks:

- customer name
- phone number
- what they purchased
- when they visited
- pending payments
- complaints
- follow-up calls

After some time:

- notebooks become messy
- data gets lost
- employees cannot track customers properly
- customer service becomes poor

So companies use a CRM system.

A CRM helps businesses manage all customer information in one place.

---

### 3. What Does CRM Actually Manage?

A CRM system manages things like:

| CRM Manages | Example |
|---|---|
| Customer details | Name, phone, email |
| Sales | Products purchased |
| Leads | New potential customers |
| Support tickets | Customer complaints |
| Tasks | Follow-up calls |
| Meetings | Sales meetings |
| Emails | Communication history |
| Reports | Monthly sales reports |

---

### 4. Why Companies Use CRM

Companies use CRM because:

#### A. Better Customer Management

They can see complete customer history in one place.

Example:

- what customer purchased
- last interaction
- pending issues

---

#### B. Improve Sales

Sales teams can:

- track leads
- follow opportunities
- close deals faster

---

#### C. Better Customer Support

Support teams can:

- track complaints
- resolve issues quickly
- improve customer satisfaction

---

#### D. Automation

CRM can automate:

- emails
- reminders
- approvals
- workflows

This reduces manual work.

---

#### E. Reporting and Analytics

Managers can view reports like:

- total sales
- monthly revenue
- employee performance
- customer growth

---

### 5. Example of CRM in Real Life

Amazon stores:

- your orders

This helps Amazon:

- recommend products

That is CRM in action.

### 9. Types of CRM

There are mainly 3 types of CRM.

#### A. Operational CRM

Focuses on daily business operations.

Example:

- sales automation
- service automation
- marketing automation

---

#### B. Analytical CRM

Focuses on data analysis.

Example:

- customer behavior analysis
- sales trends
- reporting

---

#### C. Collaborative CRM

Helps teams share customer information.

Example:

- sales team and support team sharing customer data

## Step-by-Step Practical

### Step 1: Create Free Salesforce Developer Account

Open:

https://developer.salesforce.com/signup

Fill:

- Name
- Email
- Username
- Company

After signup:

- verify email
- login to Salesforce

---

### Step 2: Open Salesforce App Launcher

After login:

1. Click the App Launcher icon
2. Search for "Sales"
3. Open Sales App

This app is built for CRM activities.

---

### Step 3: Create an Account

In CRM:

Account means a company/business/customer organization.

Steps:

1. Click "Accounts"
2. Click "New"
3. Enter:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Account Name | ABC Technologies |
| Phone | 9876543210 |
| Website | www.abctech.com |

4. Click Save

You created your first CRM customer company.

---

### Step 4: Create a Contact

Contact means a person working in that company.

Steps:

1. Open Contacts tab
2. Click New
3. Fill:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| First Name | Rahul |
| Last Name | Sharma |
| Email | rahul@gmail.com |
| Account Name | ABC Technologies |

4. Click Save

Now you linked a person with a company.

This is core CRM functionality.

---

### Step 5: Create a Lead

Lead means a potential customer.

Steps:

1. Open Leads tab
2. Click New
3. Fill details:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| First Name | Amit |
| Last Name | Verma |
| Company | XYZ Pvt Ltd |
| Phone | 9999999999 |

4. Click Save

Now Salesforce tracks potential customers.

---

### Step 6: Observe CRM Relationship

Now notice:

- Accounts = Companies
- Contacts = People
- Leads = Potential customers

This is the foundation of CRM.

### 13. Important Interview Definition

CRM is a technology and strategy used by organizations to manage customer relationships, sales, services, communication, and business processes in a centralized system.
