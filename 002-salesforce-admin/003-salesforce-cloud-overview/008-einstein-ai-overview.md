## Einstein AI — Complete Overview from Scratch

### 1. What is Einstein AI?

Einstein AI is Salesforce's artificial intelligence platform.

It brings AI capabilities directly into Salesforce products such as:

```text
Sales Cloud

Service Cloud

Marketing Cloud

Commerce Cloud

Experience Cloud

Data Cloud
```

In simple words:

```text
CRM Stores Data
      ↓
Einstein Understands Data
      ↓
Provides Predictions
Recommendations
Automation
Generative AI
```

---

### 2. Why Was Einstein AI Created?

Imagine a company has:

```text
10 Customers
```

A salesperson can manually analyze everything.

Now imagine:

```text
1 Million Customers

50 Million Activities

Millions Of Emails

Thousands Of Opportunities
```

Humans cannot manually analyze all this information.

AI helps discover patterns and provide intelligent recommendations.

---


```text

Marketing Cloud
```

